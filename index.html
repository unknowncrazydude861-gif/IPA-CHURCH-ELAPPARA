Who we are

Part of the Indian Pentecostal Assembly family

IPA Church Elappara is a local congregation of the Indian Pentecostal Assembly, meeting in the tea and cardamom hill country of Idukki district, Kerala. Our life together is built around prayer, the Word, and worship — carried in both Malayalam and Tamil so that the communities of these highlands can gather as one body.

Whether you've worshipped with us for years or you're passing through Kerala's high ranges for the first time, you're welcome at IPA Church. Come as you are — there's room for your story here.

“Worship the Lord in the beauty of holiness.”

This is the line displayed over our stage every Sunday — Psalm 29:2 — and the heart of everything we gather to do.
